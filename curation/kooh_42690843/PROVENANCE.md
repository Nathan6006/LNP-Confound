**The check worth knowing about.** SI p.S5 is a fully vector ChemDraw figure.
ChemDraw emits one stroked line per heavy-atom bond and a second parallel line
per double bond, and draws no bond into a label glyph, so

```
drawn_lines(block) == heavy_atom_bonds(SMILES) + double_bonds(kekulized)
```

is an exact identity. The page carries 483 stroked line items; 2 are the dashed
group dividers; the remaining **481 partition into exactly 48 connected
clusters, one per block, and the 48 predicted bond counts sum to 481**. Nobody
printed that number. A single wrong chain length, ring size or branch anywhere in
the 48 structures breaks the closure, and the per-block column localises it. The
same identity reproduces the 9 drawn product structures on SI pp.S12–S19
(21/42/36/32/40/58/58/72 lines, 8 pages, all exact) and the 5 drawn intermediates
on pp.S8–S11.

**The thing mass could not have told us.** The coupling is a 427 nm
photo-Petasis multicomponent reaction. N-alkylation and C-alkylation of the
intermediate imine are isomers with identical formula and identical exact mass,
so **all nine HRMS values pass either way** — demonstrated, not asserted:
`stage03_enumerate.py --seed-defect` builds `A2B1C1` with the cyclopentyl on the
nitrogen and the HRMS check passes at +0.026 mDa while the connectivity check
fires. The C-alkylation is pinned by the authors' own IUPAC names
("1-cyclopentyl**nonyl**", "tridecan-5-yl", "heptadecan-9-yl",
"tetradecane-6,1-diyl"), by the product skeleton names, and by the one-proton
N-CH methine at 2.9–3.0 ppm in every ¹H NMR.
